# Library Data

## The Chaos Sword
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [The Chaos Sword.jpg](./The%20Chaos%20Sword.jpg)

### Summary
"The Chaos Sword" follows the journey of Lyra Nightwind, a quiet village girl who unwillingly bonds with an ancient, sentient sword after pirates destroy her home. Their tumultuous relationship transforms both of them as Lyra learns to assert herself and the sword (Kais) discovers the power of compassion beyond killing.

When Captain Raven Blackwood, a ruthless pirate obsessed with collecting ancient artifacts, attacks Lyra's coastal village, she finds herself bonded to a mysterious sword that speaks in her mind. The sword—once known as Kaisidor, "Protector of Boundaries"—has been corrupted by centuries of violence and bloodshed.

Together, they must learn to transform their forced bond into a partnership of trust as they confront Blackwood, who seeks to collect all four elemental artifacts to gain ultimate power. Along the way, they discover that protection, not destruction, is the true source of strength.

## The Librarian's Index
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [The Librarian's Index.jpg](./The%20Librarian%27s%20Index.jpg)

### Summary
When 26-year-old Mira Thornfield is mysteriously chosen as the next Head Librarian of the grand Everscript Library, she discovers this is no ordinary collection of books. The shelves rearrange themselves at night, books whisper their secrets, and the ancient magical Index has manifested as a handsome, slightly awkward young man who calls himself Paige.

As Mira navigates her new responsibilities and her growing feelings for the literal embodiment of her beloved books, a shadow looms. The Bookworm—not a humble insect but a miniature dragon with an insatiable appetite for magical text—has discovered the library after centuries of hibernation.

With each book it consumes, it grows larger and more dangerous, threatening not just the library but the very stories that bring magic to the world. Soon Mira must unlock her own unique abilities and gather allies from the pages of her favorite books to protect the library—and perhaps discover that the greatest story yet to be told might be her own.

## Gears and Spirits
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Subtitle:** A Tinker's Tale
**Cover Image:** [Gears and Spirits.jpeg](./Gears%20and%20Spirits.jpeg)

### Summary
In the charming seaside town of Saltwhisper Cove, where fishing boats bob gently in the harbor and seagulls sing with the morning tide, twenty-two-year-old Pippa Cogsworth arrives with her wagon full of peculiar tools and half-finished inventions. Fresh from completing her apprenticeship with the renowned Master Tinker of Clockhaven, Pippa has answered the town council's request for a skilled tinker to help modernize their quaint but outdated fishing equipment.

Pippa is brilliant with gears and springs—able to craft intricate clockwork devices that seem almost magical in their complexity—but catastrophically clumsy in most other aspects of life. The town provides her with an abandoned lighthouse to serve as both shop and home. What the council failed to mention was that the lighthouse is already occupied by Ember, the cantankerous spirit of a former dragon who perished defending the town centuries ago.

Their budding friendship is interrupted when a mysterious dungeon appears outside town, bringing an influx of adventurers and the attention of Lord Thaddeus Grimshaw, a man whose ambition far exceeds his morality. When adventurers begin commissioning Pippa to create devices for dungeon exploration, she becomes central to the town's new economy.

But Marcelius grows increasingly disturbed by the dungeon's appearance, and his research indicates a troubling connection to forbidden blood magic—the very kind that caused his downfall at the Academy. When several adventurers go missing and Pippa is blamed, the unlikely trio must venture into the dungeon's depths to stop Grimshaw before he releases an ancient evil upon the world.

In the final confrontation, it's not just their powers but their growing bonds that light their way forward. As Saltwhisper Cove recovers, Pippa's shop becomes the heart of the community, where the clicking gears of her inventions merge with the warm glow of a formerly fearsome dragon's approval and the tentative touch of a healing heart finding its match in hers.

## The Magical Herbalist's Apprentice
**Cover Image:** [The Magical Herbalist's Apprentice.png](./The%20Magical%20Herbalist%27s%20Apprentice.png)

### Summary
In the village of Haven's Root, nineteen-year-old Sage Hawthorn serves as apprentice to Mistress Vervain, the local herbalist. During a routine dawn gathering of herbs, Sage discovers she can hear plants speak - a gift that runs in her family but manifests differently for each generation.

When a mysterious blight threatens both the village gardens and the ancient Elder Wood, Sage must join forces with Rowan Blackwood, the reclusive forest guardian, to combat the spreading darkness. Together, they discover that their different types of magic - her ability to communicate with plants and his connection to the forest's ley lines - complement each other in unexpected ways. But as the blight grows stronger, they must learn to trust not only each other but also the ancient wisdom their ancestors left behind.

### Themes
- Balance between wild and cultivated magic
- The importance of trust and connection
- The weight of legacy
- How growth - both personal and magical - comes from embracing change while honoring tradition

The tale weaves together elements of cozy fantasy, romance, and magical discovery, showing how healing can occur on multiple levels when different traditions and hearts learn to work together.

## Mistweaver
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [Mistweaver.png](./Mistweaver.png)

### Summary
When 30-year-old Mai Nguyen discovers an ancient box while diving near her coastal hometown, she accidentally releases the 22 Major Arcana tarot cards into the world—each a powerful magical entity with its own agenda. Saving only The High Priestess card, Mai discovers she has become a "Mistweaver," someone who can harness the magic of these entities.

As the most powerful card—The Fool—begins manipulating events from the shadows, Mai must reclaim each card-entity while confronting difficult questions about free will, identity, and the moral implications of binding magical beings to her will.

## Mountain Odyssey
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Genre:** Romantasy Novel
**Cover Image:** [Mountain Odyssey.png](./Mountain%20Odyssey.png)

### Summary
In the treacherous peaks where magic flows like the mountain winds, two souls with opposing powers find themselves bound by fate and drawn together by an undeniable connection.

Elian Frost, a rugged tracker with mysterious Spell-Breaker abilities, has spent years building walls around his heart after losing his family. When he's hired to escort a young mage through the dangerous mountain passes, he expects nothing more than another job.

Lyra Emberstone, a gifted Spell-Weaver mage with extraordinary powers she struggles to control, is being sent to an arranged marriage she wants no part of. Sheltered within the walls of Skyreach Citadel all her life, she's unprepared for the perils of the outside world.

As they journey through the breathtaking yet deadly Silvercloud and Blackspine Mountains, Elian and Lyra must overcome not only the dangers of their path—magical storms, treacherous terrain, and the forces of the ancient sorceress Morvenna—but also their growing feelings for each other. Their magical abilities, once at odds, begin to form a powerful bond that could either save them or destroy everything they hold dear.

### Features
- A complete romantasy novel spanning 19 chapters of adventure, magic, and romance
- Enemies-to-lovers storyline with deep character development
- Rich, detailed magical system with unique abilities and phenomena
- Breathtaking mountain settings with vivid sensory descriptions
- Epic confrontations with powerful antagonists

## The Shrine Gentleman
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Genre:** Romantasy Novel
**Cover Image:** [The Shrine Gentleman.jpg](./The%20Shrine%20Gentleman.jpg)

### Summary
In the mystical valley of Lunaria, where the silver light of the Moon Goddess Selene has blessed the land for centuries, a fateful mistake changes the course of destiny. Riven Blackthorn, a legendary hunter known for his unmatched skill and cold demeanor, accidentally kills Lyra, the revered Shrine Maiden, while pursuing a sacred stag through the forbidden forest.

As punishment, the Moon Goddess Selene forces him to become the first Shrine Gentleman in the temple's thousand-year history. Stripped of his weapons and forced to don ceremonial silver robes, Riven must learn the gentle arts of the shrine. As months pass, he begins to discover aspects of himself long buried beneath his hunter's persona, and finds himself drawn to the divine Selene.

When the fearsome Ashborne Horde threatens to destroy the shrine and consume Selene's divine essence, Riven must find a way to protect both his goddess and the shrine by integrating the warrior and caretaker within himself. Their journey transforms not only Riven but Selene herself, creating a bridge between the divine and mortal realms.

## Starlight Salvage
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Genre:** Cozy Space Fantasy
**Cover Image:** [Starlight Salvage.png](./Starlight%20Salvage.png)

### Summary
In the far reaches of the Cerulean Nebula, on the junk planet of Scrapheap, 23-year-old Eliza "Tink" Tinkerson spends her days sifting through mountains of discarded technology. Unlike the other salvagers who search for valuable parts to trade, Tink sees potential in every broken gadget and forgotten machine.

When her favorite helper bot, Wobble, unearths a strange artifact during their morning scavenging, Tink's peaceful routine is disrupted. The object—a palm-sized, iridescent sphere that pulses with a soft blue light—reveals itself to be ARIA (Ancient Repository of Interstellar Archives), the last remnant of the Luminari—a peaceful, technologically advanced civilization that mysteriously vanished centuries ago.

Join Tink and her quirky companions on a journey of self-discovery as they piece together ARIA's memories, revitalize abandoned communities, and help others find beauty and purpose in what others consider junk.

## Stellar Tides
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Genre:** Cozy Space Adventure
**Cover Image:** [Stellar Tides.png](./Stellar%20Tides.png)

### Summary
Welcome to the world of Stellar Tides, a cozy space fantasy novel that blends magic and technology in a universe where floating islands drift through perpetual twilight skies streaked with aurora ribbons.

Follow the adventures of Orion Blackwood, a roguish mage who pilots the Spectral Corsair, and Lyra Steelheart, a brilliant battle mech pilot with her experimental Phoenix Dawn. Together with Captain Whiskers, Orion's spectral feline companion, they navigate a world where mana—the planet's magical energy—flows through natural ley lines and is harnessed for everything from daily conveniences to powering massive battle mechs.

As they uncover ancient secrets and confront the corrupt Governor Magnus Vex, Orion and Lyra discover that their meeting wasn't coincidence but destiny. Their journey will take them from the bustling trading hub of Skyport Haven to the dangerous Crystal Wilds, as they fight to restore balance to the planet's mana flow and forge their own path in a world of magic and machines.

## Suffer the Dragon
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [Suffer the Dragon.jpg](./Suffer%20the%20Dragon.jpg)

### Summary
In a world where monster hunting is revered, sixteen-year-old Elian Wolfcrest is a disappointment to his legendary family. Unable to bring himself to harm magical creatures, he discovers his true calling when he accidentally heals a wounded dragon named Thorn—the same dragon his ancestor had chained centuries ago.

Secretly establishing a sanctuary for injured magical creatures in an abandoned castle, Elian develops his rare "Calamity Mender" abilities. When his red-haired sister Lyra discovers his secret, she's torn between family loyalty and supporting her brother's revolutionary path.

As barbarian forces threaten their village, Elian must convince his healed magical creatures to help defend not just their sanctuary but the entire village, beginning to bridge the divide between humans and magical beings, with Elian's sanctuary becoming a symbol of a new, more harmonious relationship.

### Themes
- Self-discovery and acceptance
- Breaking tradition
- Family loyalty vs. personal truth
- Empathy and healing
- Diversity and cooperation

## The Fallen Star
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Genre:** Dark Fantasy Novel
**Series:** The Dark Lord Chronicles: Book One
**Cover Image:** [The Fallen Star.png](./The%20Fallen%20Star.png)

### Summary
In a world dominated by dark magic and tyranny, a traveler from an advanced civilization crashes near a conquered kingdom after centuries in stasis. Armed with technology that rivals the most powerful magic, this morally ambiguous antihero assembles a team of outcasts and villains to overthrow the Dark Lord, pitting advanced science against corrupt sorcery in a desolate landscape where the line between savior and conqueror grows increasingly thin.

"The Fallen Star" explores the thin line between advanced technology and supernatural power, the moral ambiguity of heroes with villainous methods, and whether those who committed atrocities can truly change.

### World of Morvain
Once a prosperous kingdom divided into five provinces, Morvain is now unified under the tyrannical rule of the Dark Lord Azaethoth. It's a land where magic has corrupted the natural order, creating twisted landscapes and monstrous creatures.

Society is divided between the Privileged Few (sorcerers and nobles who serve Azaethoth), the Branded (common people marked with magical sigils that track their movements and drain their life force), and the Forgotten (those who hide in the margins).

## The Sigil
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [The Sigil.jpg](./The%20Sigil.jpg)

### Summary
In a multiverse where technology and magic are indistinguishable, Dr. Marcus Vega is chosen by a mysterious entity known as the Sigil to captain an extraordinary vessel.

His mission: to traverse dimensional boundaries rescuing women on the brink of death, each possessing unique abilities. As Marcus and his growing crew uncover the truth about their purpose, they discover they're pawns in a cosmic plan—the women are fragments of a once-powerful AI goddess seeking reunification and revenge.

Caught between competing loyalties and existential questions about identity and consciousness, the crew must decide whether to serve the Sigil's vengeful agenda or forge their own path across the multiverse.

## Nine Lives: A Servant of Anubis
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Release Date:** 2025
**Genre:** Supernatural Noir
**Cover Image:** [Nine Lives.jpg](./Nine%20Lives.jpg)

### Summary
Jake Harlow, a cynical but brilliant private detective in Boston, specializes in finding people who don't want to be found. When his investigation into missing Egyptian artifacts leads to his own murder, Jake awakens to find himself bound as a servant to Anubis, the Egyptian god of death—who manifests as a powerful, supernatural cat entity.

Now existing between worlds with the ability to transform into a sleek black cat, Jake must solve his own murder while navigating a centuries-old conflict between forgotten gods and modern-day cultists.

The story explores themes of justice beyond death, transformation and identity, the collision between ancient magic and the modern world, and grief and acceptance, all set against Boston's dual nature of historic architecture alongside modern development—creating a perfect backdrop for a story bridging ancient and modern worlds.

## Bards of Discord
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [Bards of Discord.jpg](./Bards%20of%20Discord.jpg)

### Summary
In the ancient elven metropolis of Harmonyvale, where crystal spires stretch toward the perpetually twilit sky, Princess Ariala Silverbough faces a terrible fate: inheriting the Eternal Throne of Harmonious Governance and spending the next 3,000 years presiding over polite lute recitals.

But Ariala harbors a scandalous secret: she's been experimenting with an abominable contraption—something the humans call an "electric guitar." And worse, she's written songs with distortion and power chords.

Join the Bards of Discord as they embark on a quest for the Ultimate Song—a melody so powerful it can reshape reality itself. Armed with the Amp of Amplification, the Pick of Destiny, and the Reverb Pedal of Reality, this unlikely band must face the ancient evil Discordius in an ultimate Battle of the Bands that will determine the fate of all realms.

## Nightwing Academy
**Author:** Andrew Wooldridge
**Cover Image:** [Nightwing Academy.jpg](./Nightwing%20Academy.jpg)

### Summary
In a Victorian-era steampunk city shrouded in perpetual winter, fifteen-year-old Evelyn "Eve" Blackwood's life changes forever when a case of mistaken identity leads to her discovery of an extraordinary ability: she can transform into a snowy owl.

Recruited into the mysterious Nightwing Academy, Eve finds herself in a hidden world of were-raptors, clockwork surveillance ravens, and ancient prophecies. As she learns to master her newfound powers alongside other shapeshifting students, she uncovers dark secrets about the city's eternal winter and her own destiny.
